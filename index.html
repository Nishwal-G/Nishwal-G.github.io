Welcome to my Homepage!

Welcome to my website. I am a physicist interested in how complex collective behaviour emerges from microscopic physical rules. My research spans quantum materials, statistical physics, biological physics, and computational modelling—from neutron-scattering studies of frustrated quantum magnets to polymer simulations of chromatin organisation and cellular memory. Having recently completed an MPhys in Physics at the University of Edinburgh, my next research will focus on collective ciliary dynamics, active matter, and biological information processing at the University of Cambridge.

This site brings together my research, publications, computational projects, and wider academic interests. I have tried to make it clear and accessible, including for readers with colour-vision deficiencies and reading difficulties. If you notice anything that could make the site more inclusive or easier to use, please feel free to get in touch.
